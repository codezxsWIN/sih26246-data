# Draft for the SIH problem owner or NCS/DGE

Subject: SIH26246 - Request for approved NCS vacancy data access

We are participating in SIH26246 and building a labour-market intelligence prototype, starting with Maharashtra/Pune and the IT/ITES, automotive and electronics sectors.

We have acquired the published NCS dashboard statistics and statistical metadata. We need an approved read-only feed or anonymized export of individual vacancies to demonstrate current skill demand accurately.

Please provide the authorized API/base URL, schema/OpenAPI documentation, credentials or sandbox onboarding, pagination/rate limits, permissible storage/reuse terms and refresh frequency. If API onboarding is unavailable during the hackathon, a CSV/JSON export for Maharashtra/Pune would allow us to proceed.

Requested vacancy fields: stable job ID, source/partner, employer name or anonymized employer ID, title, description, skills, sector/occupation codes, qualification, experience, salary with unit, number of openings, state/district/city, posting date, expiry date, active/closed status and last-modified timestamp. Please identify fields that are unavailable.

Please also confirm whether historical snapshots or change feeds are available, how partner duplicates and multi-location vacancies should be counted, and the definitions used for vacancies mobilised versus active vacancies.

We do not need personal jobseeker records or candidate contact details. State/district/occupation aggregates for jobseeker supply would be sufficient.
