# Third-party data and attribution

The MIT licence applies to original collector/extractor code. It does not relicense downloaded government publications, qualification documents, website software or job postings.

| Source | Recorded usage position |
|---|---|
| NCS | Government-owned public statistics and metadata. Attribute NCS/DGE/MoLE. No blanket open bulk-vacancy licence was established. Raw site code and documents are local-only. |
| data.gov.in | Resource-level terms control. NDSAP release is stated for the NCS catalogue; confirm the applicable Government Open Data Licence and exceptions before redistribution. |
| MoSPI PLFS | Public metadata/reports; unit-level access requires a free account and the portal's conditions. Do not distribute microdata obtained under a user account without checking those conditions. |
| AISHE | Official public report, attribute Ministry of Education. Institution microdata availability/redistribution were not established. |
| Census | Official historical tables and publications; preserve Census attribution, codes, year and source definitions. |
| PMKVY/MSDE | Official public administrative tables. Preserve scheme, period, measure and MSDE attribution. No candidate-level data collected. |
| NCO-2015 | DGE official classification. Preserve official codes and source attribution. |
| DGT/DVET | Attribute government training authorities. Organizational references and grading are not learner microdata. Staff-contact-bearing historical PDFs remain local-only, excluded from distribution. |
| NIC-2008 | Official MoSPI industry classification; retain edition and codes, and match the classification used by each survey. |
| PIB NAPS/NATS | Official administrative engagement aggregates for calendar2025. Preserve scheme, reference year and source. |
| UDISE+/OpenCity | Third-party public mirror of government school aggregates. Mirror catalogue lists Other (Public Domain); primary reuse conditions not independently verified. Attribute UDISE+ and OpenCity. No pupil/teacher personal records collected. |
| NSDC/SSC/NQR | Qualification standards retain publisher/document rights. Public availability is not an unrestricted redistribution grant. |
| Kaggle fallback | Catalogue lists CC BY-SA 4.0 for Shreyash Gade's `shree0910/india-tech-job-market-2026-23k-records`. Preserve attribution and share-alike terms if distributing adaptations. Original Naukri posting rights were not independently established. Posting files remain local-only. |

Do not convert an official-source status into a claim that every employer advertisement is verified, representative or current.
